Data Center Knowledge reports that data-center projects are taking longer to reach construction because developers cannot get electricity connections or local approval as fast as they can announce a site. Projects that broke ground between late 2025 and late 2026 waited just over two years from announcement, longer than projects that started in the five years before. Analysts quoted in the piece name the grid and public opposition as the two hardest constraints, and they note the mismatch in clocks: a data center can be built in one to three years, while new grid infrastructure takes five to fifteen.
