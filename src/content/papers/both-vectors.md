A story about AI is usually filed under hope or harm before the facts are read. This site files it under both until the facts choose.

The method has four moves.

1. Name the shared fact. A watt, a patient, a statute, a model release, a queue.
2. Ask the Acheron question. What is kept alive, who is ranked below it, who holds the lock, and what happens when the ranking is wrong?
3. Ask the Lyra question. What gets lighter, who receives it, what skill leaves the person, and can they take it back?
4. Mark the coordinate. Write the sentence where both readings are forced to talk about the same arrangement.

The method does not split the difference into a mush. A fact that supports only one reading should be marked that way. A story that survives only one of the questions is still worth keeping. Say so in the note.

## Three patterns

**A campus that wants power.** Acheron asks who loses electricity, water, or a vote when the load arrives, and who writes the order of the cutoff. Lyra asks whether the same load buys scientific and medical work the region could not do, and whether the campus can drop its demand on the evening peak so homes stay cool. Both questions can be true of one substation. The coordinate is the megawatt that is either a private load or a public reserve.

**A model in a clinic.** Lyra asks whether the patient is seen sooner, with fewer dead ends. Acheron asks whether the model has become the hearing, whether a person can refuse it, and whether a clinician can still contradict it. A gain in accuracy that cannot be appealed is a gain and a lock. The coordinate is the moment a suggestion becomes the decision.

**A review before a model is released.** Acheron asks whether the public has lost leverage over a system that will sort them, and whether the review is a closed room. Lyra asks whether catching a dangerous capability before it ships is the shield doing the job it was built for. A review no one can read fails both questions. It is neither an appealable lock nor an inspectable shield.

## How a signal is written

The [signals](/signals) are this method in short form. Each one links to the source and keeps the article there. The note names the vector, or says the story belongs to both, and it points at the coordinate. New essays join the papers as new files. They do not need a new page design. The long form of the two questions lives in [Acheron](/acheron) and [Lyra](/lyra).
