Acheron starts from a practical problem. Bodies, cities, and hearings are slow. A failing grid, a cascading model, or a hospital past its capacity does not wait. A system built to keep the core alive will rank what stays on. That ranking is triage. When a model does the ranking, the sort can finish before the people inside it have spoken.

The picture is cold on purpose. Power, water, shipping, emergency rooms, and the machines now inside all of them have hard limits. Past the limit, a plea is not a plan. This reading says the response will look like a lock on the controls. Fewer hands on the switch. A narrower set of exceptions. A preference for the continuity of the system over the preference of any one person in it. The line from the manifesto is the short version. To keep core infrastructure alive, the controls are locked from the inside.

## What the lock costs

**Leverage.** A person who cannot see the ranking cannot appeal it. Credit, a place in a clinic, a queue for power or water, a job screened before a human looks up. The harm is the missing hearing.

**Concentration.** The models that would do this work are expensive. They gather where electricity, chips, and capital already gather. A lock held by a few firms and a few agencies is a lock everyone else rents.

**Speed.** Biological and political reaction times are the constraint this reading keeps pointing at. A person can notice a wrong decision and still be too late to reverse it. Systems that update in seconds train their operators to trust the update.

**Brittleness.** A rule that is efficient for the grid can be cruel, and a cruel rule that is automated is hard to embarrass into changing. An optimizer has an objective. It does not have shame.

## The benefit inside the warning

Leaving the benefit out would turn the paper into a poster. Panic is a bad operator of a power grid. A city under heat or storm needs a rule, written in advance, for who keeps current. Some of those rules should be public, debated while there is time, and then carried out without a fresh argument in the dark. Acheron becomes a warning at a specific moment: when the rule can no longer be read by the people under it, or when the objective is the survival of the machine rather than the survival of the people.

## Four questions for a story

Ask them of any signal before you decide it is only a risk or only a benefit.

1. What is being kept alive?
2. Who is ranked below it?
3. Who can open the lock?
4. What happens to a person the ranking gets wrong?
